## 1. Use of artificial intelligence on the website

### 1.1. Description of AI application

The e-dietetyk.com website uses artificial intelligence systems (hereinafter: "AI") as a tool to assist in the process of creating individual nutrition plans. AI generates preliminary nutrition plan proposals based on data obtained from the dietary interview completed by the User.

### 1.2. AI model used

The website uses language models from the GPT-4 family provided by OpenAI LLC (San Francisco, USA) via an application programming interface (API).

### 1.3. Role of AI vs. role of the dietitian

**Artificial intelligence serves solely as a supporting tool** — it generates a preliminary nutrition plan proposal, which serves as a starting point for further work by the specialist.

**The dietitian (a human) makes the final decision** — every plan generated by AI is reviewed, modified if necessary, and approved by a qualified dietitian before being made available to the User. The dietitian bears professional responsibility for the final content of the plan.

No nutrition plan is made available to the User without prior review and approval by a dietitian.

The above is in compliance with the transparency requirements set out in Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 June 2024 laying down harmonised rules on artificial intelligence (the AI Act), in particular art. 50 on transparency obligations.

## 2. What data is processed by AI

### 2.1. Data from the dietary interview

To generate a nutrition plan proposal, only **anonymised** data from the dietary interview is transmitted to the AI system, in particular:

- age, sex, height, body weight,
- level of physical activity,
- nutrition goals (e.g., weight loss, muscle mass gain),
- allergies and food intolerances,
- dietary preferences (e.g., vegetarianism, veganism),
- chronic diseases and medications taken (only to the extent relevant for dietary purposes),
- other information provided by the User in the interview form.

**Data identifying the User (name, surname, e-mail address, phone number) is NOT transmitted to the AI system.** Before sending a query to AI, the data is anonymised on the server side — the AI system has no ability to identify a specific User.

### 2.2. Food product data

AI uses a Polish food product database containing nutritional information (energy values, macronutrients, micronutrients). This database does not contain any personal data.

## 3. How the AI-assisted nutrition plan creation process works

The process of creating a nutrition plan using AI consists of the following stages:

### Step 1: Completing the health interview
The User completes a detailed dietary interview form, providing information about their health condition, dietary preferences, allergies, goals, and lifestyle.

### Step 2: Calculating nutrition targets
Based on the interview data, the system automatically calculates individual nutrition targets, including caloric requirements, macronutrient distribution (protein, fat, carbohydrates), and other dietary parameters. Calculations are based on recognised scientific formulas (including Harris-Benedict, Mifflin-St Jeor).

### Step 3: Generating a plan proposal by AI
Anonymised interview data and calculated nutrition targets are transmitted to the AI system, which generates a preliminary nutrition plan proposal. The proposal takes into account available food products from the Polish database.

### Step 4: Review and approval by a dietitian
A qualified dietitian conducts a detailed review of the AI-generated plan proposal. The dietitian:
- verifies the plan's substantive correctness,
- checks conformity with the nutrition targets and the User's health condition,
- modifies the plan if necessary (changing products, portions, meals),
- approves the final version of the plan.

### Step 5: Making the plan available to the User
After approval by the dietitian, the nutrition plan is made available to the User through the User dashboard on the website.

## 4. Limitations of artificial intelligence

The User should be aware of the following limitations of the AI system:

1. **AI can make mistakes.** Artificial intelligence systems, including language models, may generate content that is imprecise, incomplete, or inappropriate in a given clinical context. This is why every plan is reviewed by a dietitian.

2. **An AI-generated plan does not constitute medical advice.** A nutrition plan — both in its preliminary (AI) version and after review by a dietitian — does not replace medical advice. In the case of diseases, eating disorders, or health concerns, the User should consult a doctor.

3. **Review by a qualified dietitian.** Every plan generated by AI is subject to mandatory review by a dietitian with appropriate professional qualifications. The dietitian is responsible for the substantive correctness of the final version of the plan.

4. **AI does not account for non-dietary factors.** The AI system does not have knowledge of the full medical context of the User (e.g., laboratory test results, drug interactions beyond the information provided in the interview).

## 5. User rights in relation to the use of AI

The User has the following rights:

### 5.1. Right to information about the use of AI
The User has the right to receive clear information that artificial intelligence is used in the process of creating the nutrition plan, how this process works, and what data is processed by AI. This document fulfils this information obligation.

### 5.2. Right to human intervention
In accordance with art. 22(3) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR), the User has the right to obtain human intervention from the Controller. On e-dietetyk.com, human intervention is a standard part of the process — every AI plan is reviewed by a dietitian.

### 5.3. Right to contest an AI decision
The User has the right to express their point of view and contest proposals generated by AI. In such a case, the User may submit feedback to the dietitian through the platform, and the dietitian will take it into account when reviewing or modifying the plan.

### 5.4. Right to obtain a plan without the use of AI
The User has the right to request a nutrition plan prepared without the use of artificial intelligence. In such a case, the plan will be prepared manually by a dietitian. The User may submit such a request by contacting the Controller at the e-mail address indicated in this document. A manually prepared plan may involve a longer waiting time.

## 6. Data security in the context of AI

### 6.1. Data anonymisation
Data transmitted to the AI system does not contain information that could identify the User. Before sending a query to the OpenAI API, the system automatically removes identifying data such as e-mail address, first name, and surname.

### 6.2. No use of data for model training
Under the Data Processing Agreement concluded with OpenAI LLC, data transmitted via the API is not used by OpenAI for training, improving, or developing AI models. Data is processed solely for the purpose of generating a response to the query and is deleted in accordance with OpenAI's retention policy (maximum 30 days for security purposes).

### 6.3. Data transfer to the USA
Data is transferred to OpenAI LLC headquartered in the United States. The transfer is carried out on the basis of Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by a European Commission decision, ensuring an adequate level of personal data protection in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR.

### 6.4. Data encryption
Dietary interview data stored in the website's database is encrypted using the AES-256-GCM algorithm. Data transmission between the server and the OpenAI API is conducted via the TLS 1.2+ protocol.
